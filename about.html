Our Story

Kairos Influence was founded in 2022 in Observatory, Cape Town, by a collective of young Capetonian designers. The brand emerged out of the city's skate and hip-hop scenes, producing small-batch graphic tees, hoodies, and accessories that speak to bold self-expression.

The name "Kairos" comes from the Greek concept of the opportune, defining moment — reflecting our belief that personal style is about seizing the moment, not following trends.

Mission

To empower young South Africans to express their individuality through bold, locally-designed streetwear.

Vision

To become the leading independent streetwear label out of Cape Town, recognised for authentic design and community-first culture.

Meet the Crew

Kairos Influence is run by a small, close-knit crew of designers, creatives, and friends who model, shoot, and wear every piece themselves before it ever goes up for sale. What you see on the site is what we actually wear.
